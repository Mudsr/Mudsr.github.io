Services

What I can take off your plate

Three things I do end to end. Pick one, or combine them when your product needs all three.

1

Cloud-native backends and APIs

A backend that handles real traffic, real payments and real integrations without surprises.

  • REST and GraphQL APIs in NestJS, .NET or Laravel
  • Queues, workers and event-driven services that scale out
  • Stripe, Paddle, Shopify, CRM and third-party integrations
  • Multi-tenant SaaS architecture and data modelling
  • Rescuing slow or fragile legacy code

Good fit if: you are launching, scaling past the MVP, or your current backend keeps breaking.

2

Cloud infrastructure and DevOps

Infrastructure you can rebuild from code, deploy in minutes and stop worrying about.

  • AWS, GCP and Azure setups written in Terraform
  • Kubernetes, Helm, ECS, or Ansible-managed servers on Hetzner
  • CI/CD pipelines in GitHub Actions or GitLab CI
  • Security hardening, secrets and least-privilege access
  • Monitoring, alerting and cloud cost clean-up

Good fit if: deploys are manual, nobody knows how prod was set up, or the cloud bill keeps growing.

3

AI features in your product

LLM features connected to your data and your users, built to run in production, not just in a demo.

  • Python and FastAPI AI services, RAG search and chat over your data
  • AI assistants and support bots inside your app
  • Agents and automations that call your APIs and tools
  • OpenAI, Claude and open-model integrations, MCP servers
  • Guardrails, evaluation, cost and latency control

Good fit if: you want AI in your product but need it secure, reliable and affordable at scale.
